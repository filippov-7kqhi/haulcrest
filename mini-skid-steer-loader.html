Questions

About the Mini Skid Steer

What can it lift?

We are confirming the rated operating capacity with the manufacturer and will publish it here rather than estimate it. Rated capacity is conventionally half the static tipping load — be wary of any seller quoting the tipping load as the lift. Email us if you need the figure before ordering.

Will other attachments fit?

It uses a quick-attach plate with auxiliary hydraulics already run to the arm. Check the plate standard and the flow requirement of any attachment against the machine before buying it, and we will confirm compatibility in writing.

How wide is it?

We are confirming the exact track width and overall width with the manufacturer. Measure the narrowest point on your access route and we will check it against the real figure before you order.

Petrol rather than diesel?

Petrol keeps the weight down so the machine trailers easily and stays manoeuvrable in a garden. It also avoids red-diesel record-keeping for commercial use.

Is the bucket included?

Yes — the machine ships with the bucket shown in the photographs. Other attachments are separate; ask us for prices and we will quote them with your order.
