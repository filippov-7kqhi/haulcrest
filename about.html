About us

Two machines for the jobs a lorry can't reach

HaulCrest exists because access is the hard part of groundwork. The spoil is not the problem and the hardcore is not the problem — getting either through a 750 mm gap is the problem, and most of the machinery sold in the UK is either too wide for it or too small to be worth taking off the trailer.

So we stock two machines that solve exactly that: a 1-tonne tracked dumper that fits a doorway and tips high enough to clear a skip wall, and a stand-on loader narrow enough for a double gate that still runs standard skid-steer attachments. Both live in a Wakefield warehouse alongside their wear parts.

We are a supplier, not a manufacturer, and we say so. What we control is the specification, the pre-delivery inspection on every unit, the parts on the shelf, and a two-year warranty we actually honour. Where a figure has a standard behind it — rated operating capacity, gradient, payload — we quote it that way, even when a bigger number would sell better.

How we operate

Stock, not drop-ship

Machines sit in our warehouse in Wakefield. When the site says in stock, there is one on the floor with a serial number.

Inspected before it ships

Fluids, track tension, hydraulic pressures and a running test on every unit. The signed sheet travels in the crate.

Parts on the shelf

Rubber tracks, rollers, pumps, belts and filters held in the UK and dispatched within 48 hours of your call.
